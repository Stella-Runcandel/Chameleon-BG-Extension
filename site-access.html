Before you enable

ChameleonBG adds custom image or video backgrounds by changing how a website is rendered in your browser.

Some websites may restrict browser extensions or visual modifications under their terms or account rules. Review the site's policies if unsure. ChameleonBG cannot guarantee that a website permits this or that your account will never be restricted.
