    LOCAL DATA

    Privacy & Data

    Understand where your customizations live and how to manage them.

    Local-first storage

    • Images and videos are stored in extension-owned IndexedDB. Site settings and component mappings are stored in extension storage.
    • Uploaded files are not sent to ChameleonBG servers by this extension.
    • Clearing a website's cookies or website storage should not clear the extension-owned media library.
    • Disabling ChameleonBG preserves saved data. Uninstalling normally removes browser-managed extension data, but exported backups remain where you saved them.

    What site access means

    ChameleonBG stores imported media and customizations in extension-owned browser storage. It does not upload them to a ChameleonBG server or use analytics in this build.

    On a site you enable, the extension can inspect page areas and display only the media assigned to that site's saved configuration. The site may observe visual changes to its page. ChameleonBG does not access or change protected video streams.

    Review each site's rules before enabling access. Exported backups are files you control and may contain your imported media.
