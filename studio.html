    PERMISSIONS

    Site Access

    ChameleonBG should only run on websites you choose to enable.

    Manage website access

    Visit a website and open the ChameleonBG toolbar popup to enable or pause that site, or revoke its browser permission. Revoking access preserves saved customizations; deleting those settings is a separate action.

    Some websites may restrict visual-modification extensions. Review the site's applicable policies before enabling access. ChameleonBG cannot determine or guarantee whether a particular site's rules permit an extension.

    Access has two separate parts: browser permission allows ChameleonBG to inspect or modify the site, while ChameleonBG's site toggle controls whether it applies saved customizations. Both must be enabled. A global pause disables behavior without deleting saved media or settings.

    LOCAL DATA

    Privacy & Data

    Understand where your customizations live and how to manage them.

    Local-first storage

    • Images and videos are stored in extension-owned IndexedDB. Site settings and component mappings are stored in extension storage.
    • Uploaded files are not sent to ChameleonBG servers by this extension.
    • Clearing a website's cookies or website storage should not clear the extension-owned media library.
    • Disabling ChameleonBG pauses customizations but preserves saved data. Browser-managed extension data is normally removed when the extension is uninstalled; exported backups stored elsewhere are not.
